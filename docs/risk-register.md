# Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Limited time causes late deliverables | High | High | Prioritize critical path, review daily and protect scope | Student |
| GitHub setup or access issue | Medium | Medium | Verify repository, Issues and Projects early | Student |
| Requirements misunderstood | Medium | High | Validate all six features against assignment brief | Student |
| Prototype defects or unclear flow | Medium | High | Review user flow and execute six test cases | Student |
| Scope expansion | Medium | Medium | Use formal change control; defer if schedule impact is excessive | Student |
| Loss of project files | Low | High | Commit documents regularly and keep a local backup | Student |

## Most serious risk
Limited time is the most serious risk because the deadline is fixed and later deliverables depend on earlier planning. Mitigation: complete the charter, requirements, schedule and core mockups before optional polish.
