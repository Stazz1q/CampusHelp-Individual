# Project Charter — CampusHelp

## Business problem
University requests arrive through emails and paper forms. The fragmented process makes requests difficult to track, prioritize and assign.

## Objective
Plan and demonstrate a centralized web-based service-request portal for university students.

## Sponsor and stakeholders
- Sponsor: University Administration (simulated)
- Students
- IT support
- Classroom maintenance staff
- Library staff
- Service administrators

## Scope
**In scope:** request submission, categories, priorities, unique IDs, administrator assignment, status updates, dashboard mockups and a demonstration prototype.

**Out of scope:** production authentication, production hosting, live email/SMS, payment processing and integration with real university records.

## Duration and constraints
10-working-day planning baseline; individual academic delivery; limited time; no external service charges.

## Budget baseline
45 estimated hours × 50,000 UZS/hour = **2,250,000 UZS**. This is a simulated planning cost, not an actual payment.

## Measurable success criteria
1. All six required capabilities are represented in requirements or interface designs.
2. Twelve work items are tracked in GitHub Issues.
3. Student request and administrator dashboard flows are demonstrated.
4. Six test cases are documented and executed against the prototype.
5. Four milestone deliverable groups are reviewed.

## Assumptions
All prototype data is fictional. Notifications and persistence are simulated. No production service is claimed.
